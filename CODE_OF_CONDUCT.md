# Code of Conduct

NandTier is a learning project, and many people here are students taking their first steps in hardware. Be the person you would have wanted to meet when you were learning.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient. There are no stupid questions about how a computer works.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain *why* when you suggest a change.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content, or personal attacks, in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **nandtier.project@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or ban anyone who breaks these rules, temporarily or permanently.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
